I developed a desktop Windows application that automates the creation of Anki flashcards. The tool fetches word definitions from Google Translate, Oxford Dictionary, and Cambridge Dictionary, downloads pronunciation audio and images from Google, and integrates with several other applications to streamline the language learning process.
